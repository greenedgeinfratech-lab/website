What We Do

We are committed to providing high-quality solar power solutions for your home or business. Our team of experienced professionals will work with you to design and install a customized solar power system that meets your specific needs and budget.

Here are some of the services we offer:

  1. Solar panel installation: We install high-quality solar panels on your property to convert sunlight into electricity for your home or business.
  2. Energy storage solutions: We help you choose the right battery system to store solar energy for use at night or during power cuts.
  3. Maintenance and repairs: Ongoing cleaning, maintenance and repair services keep your solar system working efficiently.
  4. Subsidy and loan guidance: For eligible homes we help with the PM Surya Ghar subsidy application. Banks offer concessional solar loans under the scheme, and we help you prepare the documents.
  5. Customized design: We design each system around your energy use, roof and budget.
  6. Residential, commercial and industrial installation: Solar power solutions for homes, businesses, institutions and factories across Western Uttar Pradesh.
  7. Energy efficiency consulting: Energy audits and practical advice to reduce your usage and bills.
