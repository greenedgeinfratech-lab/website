About Us

Building sustainable infrastructure solutions with innovation, reliability, and excellence.

GreenEdge Infratech was founded by an eminent Philanthropist, President of UDAAN Society, Dr. Gyanendra Mishra.

Established in 2016, Greenedge Infratech Pvt. Ltd. is a Sustainability expert assisting its Clients and partners in planning, designing and implementing innovative Green solutions to our pressing problems. Our Clients include Architects, City Planners, Builders & Developers, Multinational companies and other end users. We are dedicated to technologies that are efficient, eco-friendly, and inspired by nature. As stewards of the environment, we strongly believe sustainable development is the need of the hour and only a collective and determined effort could bring the change that we all aspire to see.

We have been providing cutting edge technology and customer friendly consultancy, along with execution of projects.

We would like to help you create sustainable and vibrant living and working spaces. We are proud of meeting and exceeding the expectations of our clients in terms of customer service, project schedule and end product. Our esteemed clientele includes educational institutions, hospitals, multinational companies as well as central and state government agencies. We employ a wide-ranging suite of robust best practice policies, and are committed to the safety, health, and wellbeing of our people, communities and the environment.

Greenedge Infratech Private Limited is also a leading renewable energy solutions provider, dedicated to building a sustainable future through solar power. Based in Aligarh, we specialize in the design, installation, and maintenance of high-quality rooftop solar systems for residential, commercial, and institutional clients.

As an empaneled partner under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, we actively support the Government of India’s mission to empower households with free and clean electricity. Through this initiative, Greenedge Infratech assists eligible customers in availing central subsidies, guiding them through the application, inspection, and subsidy claim process, all while ensuring seamless solar system installations.

Our commitment lies in making solar energy affordable, accessible, and hassle-free for every Indian home. With a focus on technical excellence, after-sales service, and transparent operations, we are proud to contribute to India’s green energy revolution and help families save on their power bills while reducing their carbon footprint.
